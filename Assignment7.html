Bustle of the Wasteland

Original Photo Image

Adobe Illustrated World


The Inspiration of the wasteland environment is connects directly to my creative interest and idenity like my Westward Equinox vanity plate logo, inspired by the Western Genre, influences and the post-apocalyptic that inspire my favorite media like Mad Max and Fallout. Starting from a public domain photo environment, I transformed it in Adobe Illustrator by stripping away realism and reshaping it into a stylized frontier space of three colors from adobe color wheel extract from the original image, without losing any meaningful details. The cracked road became a trail, I use three colors for the middle ground (one for the abandoned building and bricks) and foreground (meaning two colors for details for the front ground), and the sunset background gradient of many colors palette pushed the atmosphere toward western mythos. What began as a ruined was changed into a frontier less about destruction, more about survival and rebellion. This transformation turned the wasteland into more than just scenery, it became a resilience symbol and identity.
