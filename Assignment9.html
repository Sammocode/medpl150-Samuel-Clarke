The 3D bar chart, designed in Illustrator, visually compares the most popular gaming genres projected for 2025 by using bold vertical bars and respective genre-specific icons. Each bar rises from a networked background, giving the chart a dynamic, futuristic feel while the data is relevant to digital culture. Puzzle games lead with a striking 63%, towering above other genres and reflecting their widespread appeal across casual and mobile platforms. RPGs follow closely at 41%, showcasing their enduring popularity among immersive and narrative-driven players. Shooters & Action/Adventure games tie at 39%, indicating strong engagement in fast-paced and story-rich experiences. Meanwhile, Battle Royale holds a solid 35%, and genres like Sports (23%), Strategy (19%), and Idle Games (15%) round out the chart with more niche but steady interest. The 3D rendering adds depth and hierarchy, making it easy to compare genre dominance at a glance while reinforcing the infographic’s role as a trend forecasting tool for the gaming industry.
