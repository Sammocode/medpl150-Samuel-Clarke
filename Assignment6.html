Emotive Eye Designs (Illustrator)

Digitally Hand Drawn Emotive Eye V1


Geometric Digital Emotive Eye V2



These two emotive eye designs showcase contrasting digital illustration techniques that I explore to express emotion through stylized form. Digitally Hand-Drawn Emotive Eye V1 utilizes layered red and black shapes with intricate linework to evoke intensity and drawn emotion, set against a bold orange background that amplifies its excited tone. In contrast, Geometric Digital Emotive Eye V2 relies on clean symmetry and simplified shapes, with a red eye outlined in black and a vertical slit pupil reflected by a white reflection. Its gradient blue background adds depth by being cooler, more calculated in aesthetic. Together, these designs reflect how digital tools in Adobe Illustrator can be used to convey through both organic and structured approaches, offering a visual dialogue between spontaneity and precision.
