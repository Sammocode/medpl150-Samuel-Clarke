My Inspiration for these Collage Pieces

The Glyph Drift series, also known as Frutiger: Nature vs Corporate Technology, explores sociopolitical and ecological tension through collage compositions that resist cohesion and use negative space intentionally. The first piece, Glyph Drift: The Grid Holds, presents a vertical composition of an war conflict between Nature Girl (a superheroine) vs. Nate Wilkins (a corrupted CEO) fighting and defending their respective worlds to see who would be the dominant force, from moving forward, using layered imagery to examine how visuals behave in forced proximity. I used classic gradient blending (downplayed blending modes) with added background layers to support the theme of nature’s creative freedom versus corporate confinement. Frutiger Aero creative nature is an aesthetic that promotes technology existing in harmony with the environment, symbolizing a futuristic and optimistic utopia. Prevalent in the mid-2000s, this visual style blends natural imagery with tech-inspired elements like glossy textures and translucency. Oppose to "Corporate Memphis" is a visual style that stands in by prioritizing simplicity and functionality over realism and emotional depth, the flat design is often criticized for being generic, uninspired, and "soulless". Sourcing assets was challenging; I definitely relied on public domain stock images found online, either downloaded and/or screenshot, also using Photoshop’s Select --> Subject --> CTRL + J shortcut to isolate subjects and place them into the composition across both pieces. With 14 layers in total, this first collage is simpler than the second.

Glyph Drift 2: Interface in the System, the Sequel continues the series’ by expand upon the idea of how images behave when forced into proximity, using a more complex, horizontal composition with 36 layers in total overall. The top half features Blob Blue Guy maintaining peace and hope within his world looking forward into a bright future and treat his people well, while the corporate bottom descends into chaos corrupted by greed, filth, and AI Generated Nonsense, ruled by a Robot Devil (Shadow Demon)(disguised) abusing his people to make them miserable as possible in corporate hell. I blended assets to match their respective worlds, (Downplayed Masking) using a wide range of Photoshop blending modes from Darken, Multiply, Color, Linear Blur, Lighten, Screen, Overlay, Hard Mix, and Light, as well as Soft, Hard, Vivid, Linear, Pin, Difference, Exclusion, Subtract, Hue, Saturation, and Luminosity, depending on each image’s role. Most assets were sourced through Google searches, including various stock sites, such as Frutiger Aero Archive, Adobe Stock, Raw Pixel, Alamy, Shutterstock, FreePix, Getty Images, iStock, and others. When downloads weren’t possible, I purposely used screenshots as a workaround. I also avoided AI-generated content through exclusion filters. Despite the challenge of world building these two piece together under a tight three-week deadline of a concept, vision, and originality, but the process was worth it to express my perspective on sociopolitical and ecological issues through collage illustration story.
